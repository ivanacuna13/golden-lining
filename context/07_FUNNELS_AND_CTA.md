# 07 - FUNNELS AND CTA (Golden Lining)

## THE FUNNEL (long, give-first)

Video (credibility + genuine help) → viewer learns to think like an operator
→ relationship compounds over years → viewer becomes someone Kush does deals
with → equity/upside participation.

There is no lead magnet, no email capture, no sales call. The "conversion"
is a relationship that matures over 5-10 years. Do not bolt a guru funnel
onto this channel — it would destroy the positioning.

## CTA RULES

- **Default CTA: none or soft.** End on the idea, not a pitch. The channel's
  job is credibility and reach, not capture.
- **Allowed soft CTAs:** "If you're building something real, I read every
  comment" / subscribe for the next operator breakdown. Relationship, not
  transaction.
- **Never:** DM keywords, calculator lead magnets, "book a call," course
  mentions, investment pitches, anything that smells like the Mansoor
  pipeline. Different client, different game.
- **Sponsor/integration:** none planned. Keep it clean.

## DESCRIPTION BOX

Use for: chapter markers, the golden-lining mentality line, and (when

operator." No links except social profiles.
