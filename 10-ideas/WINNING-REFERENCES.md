## How another agent should use this database

This is a **template library**, not a topic library. The winning videos are from
other niches (coaching, agencies, AI, content) — their topics are irrelevant.
What transfers is the *packaging*: the title structure and the thumbnail pattern
that already earned the click.

**To generate a new video idea from this database:**

1. **Start from a viewer problem**, not a topic. Read
   `../context/03_PAIN_POINTS_AND_OBJECTIONS.md`. If the idea doesn't solve a
   specific pain or objection, it isn't ready — it's just information.
2. **Pick the template whose shape fits the problem.** Transformation stories →
   templates 2, 5, 9, 10. Contrarian reframes → 1, 7. Systems and methods → 4, 6.
   Opportunity/curiosity → 3, 8.
3. **Study the winning reference before writing.** Watch the video (links above).
   Read its thumbnail in `reference-thumbnails/`: who is in frame, what the text
   overlay says, how many words, where the contrast/arrow/facial expression does
   the work. Name the mechanism out loud before adapting it.
4. **Extract the template as fill-in-the-blank slots, then fill them.** Fill slots
   ONLY with facts Kush stated on the strategy call, or facts verifiable in
   `../CHANNEL_BRAIN.md` and `../transcripts/`. Mark anything Kush must confirm
   or supply with ⚠️ — never ship an unverified figure, timeline, or count.
5. **Fill the template; don't paraphrase it.** The template is the proven asset.
   Rewording it into "your own version" discards what made it win.
6. **Apply the vehicle rule.** The lesson is the topic; Kush's hotel experience is
   the proof, stories, and examples *inside* the video. Hotels appear in the title
   only when the video is about Kush's own history.
7. **Write the thumbnail as an adaptation, not a copy.** Kush in frame, hotel-world
   visuals, same structural mechanism as the reference (text count, contrast, gaze).
8. **Run the checks before finalizing:**
   - One audience per video — ambitious operators (20s–early 40s) OR established
     operators/capital, never both in one promise.
   - Against the quarantine list in `../CHANNEL_BRAIN.md` (what the old channel
     did that the new direction forbids).
   - Against the verbiage rules in `../context/04_MESSAGING_AND_VERBIAGE_RULES.md`
     (e.g., "passive income" is forbidden framing).
   - Every idea states: the viewer problem, the template used, topic vs vehicle,
     what the viewer walks away with, the source of each claim, and why the
     channel needs it.

**What never transfers:** the reference video's topic, its numbers, its authority
claims. Only the packaging transfers. A template filled with invented variables
is worse than no video — flag every ⚠️ for Kush before filming.
