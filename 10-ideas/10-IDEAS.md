# Golden Lining — 10 Launch Videos, v4 (Lessons First, Hotels as the Vehicle)

## The rule for this version

Hotels are not the topic. The topics are the operator lessons Kush teaches —
debt, operations, numbers, training, work ethic, unit economics, the long path.
Hotels are the **vehicle**: the stories, proof, and examples inside each video.
Only the videos that are *about Kush's own history* name hotels in the title.

## How to read this document

1. **The viewer's problem** — a specific pain from `context/03_PAIN_POINTS_AND_OBJECTIONS.md`
   or a stated objection. No problem solved, no video.
2. **The format** — the exact title template from Ivan's Winning YT Examples
   (32 unique templates, verified 2026-09-23). Titles fill the template; they don't paraphrase it.
3. **The call** — story, fact, or framework from the 2026-09-16 strategy call.
   ✅ = Kush stated it. ⚠️ = Kush must confirm or supply before filming.

Each idea states **topic vs vehicle**, **what the viewer walks away with**,
and **why the channel needs it**. The world gets built through the videos, not exposition.

## 1. "You Don't Want a Hotel"

- **Format:** `You Don't Want a [topic]` — direct fill. (Hotel is named because it's the
  strongest concrete example of the topic, not because the video is "about hotels.")

  problems, the corporate-GM paperwork trap.
- **Problem it solves:** The viewer romanticizes ownership — prestige, passive income.
  They've never priced what it actually costs.

  plus a filter: who should never own anything, and who should.
- **Source:** Debt discussion ("an appetite to hold high debt"); 3am-problems reality;
  corporate GMs in the back office 90% of the time.
- **Verify:** ✅ All concepts from the call. No figures used.
- **Thumbnail:** Kush close-up, serious, holding a hotel keycard; "don't buy..." with arrow.
- **Why the channel needs it:** The stakes video. The world is real and hard; Kush is
  filtering, not recruiting. (Also filters out the anti-ICP.)
