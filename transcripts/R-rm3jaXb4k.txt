Kind: captions Language: en One night sitting alone in his car, frustrated, angry, heartbroken, he reached into his pocket, pulled out his checkbook, and wrote a check to himself for $10 million for acting services rendered. He dated it for 10 years into the future. Not as a joke, not as a fantasy, but as a contract with his future self. He folded that check and kept it in his wallet until it was torn and worn out. Every day he saw it, every day it reminded him, "This is who you're becoming." He wasn't waiting for the world to believe in him. He chose to believe in himself first. Success didn't arrive immediately. It came painfully and slowly. Small acting roles, random gigs, more rejection, more doubt.
