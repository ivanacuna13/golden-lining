Kind: captions Language: en Michael Jordan didn't chase greatness. He ran from humiliation. Jordan didn't use that moment to work harder for a season. He used it to rebuild who he was. Most people try to move past pain. Jordan kept it alive. Practice wasn't about improvement. It was about domination. He didn't want to win games. He wanted to remove doubt permanently. Practices were brutal. While teammates felt pressure, mistakes weren't tolerated because mistakes reopened the wound. Jordan wasn't easy to love. He wasn't warm. He wasn't safe. Connection requires vulnerability and vulnerability felt like exposure. Better to be feared than better to be dominant than understood. Pain can build greatness, but it also builds prisons. Michael Jordan teaches us something uncomfortable. Some people don't become great because they're whole. They become great because they're fractured and refuse to look away.
