Kind: captions Language: en A strict, abusive father, a home full of tension, a childhood robbed of joy. While other kids played, Walt worked, delivering newspapers at 4:00 a.m. Freezing nights, empty pockets, and hands numb, back aching, all before the sun even rose. But, inside that suffering, he held a spark. Drawing. His little escape, his secret doorway into a better into a better world. And nobody, absolutely nobody, believed in it. Walt wanted to be an artist, but the world didn't care. He was rejected by art school, rejected by newspapers, rejected by studios. At 22, he finally got hired at a newspaper. It felt like a miracle, a chance, a beginning. But, 2 months later, they fired him. The reason? He lacks imagination. He has no creative ideas. Imagine hearing that about the one thing you love most. Most people would have quit, let the world define them, but Walt wasn't most people.
