Kind: captions Language: en This is a story of Jim Carrey, who made a decision that changed his destiny forever. Jim grew up watching his family fall apart. They lived in a van, a whole family surviving in a vehicle dreaming of fame. He was dreaming of survival. At 13 years old, he was working as a janitor after school cleaning toilets, scrubbing floors, doing the jobs adults refused. He wasn't waiting for the world to believe in him. He chose to believe in himself first.
