Kind: captions Language: en rejection one after another. 12 publishers turned her away. They told her it was too long, too childish, that kids wouldn't be interested. They couldn't see the magic in her words. Can you imagine that? Holding on to your dream, pouring yourself into it, and being told 12 times it wasn't good enough. Most people would have quit, but Rowling didn't. Finally, one small publisher, Bloomsbury, took a chance, but even they weren't sure. The chairman of the company gave the first chapter to his eight-year-old daughter and she loved it. That was enough for them to print a small run of just 500 copies. And from that humble start, everything changed.
