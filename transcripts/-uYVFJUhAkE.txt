Kind: captions Language: en This is the story of Walt Disney, the boy they tried to break and the man who refused to stay broke. Walt didn't grow up in magic, he grew up in pain. A strict, abusive father, a home full of tension, and a childhood robbed of joy. While other kids played, Walt worked. Delivering newspapers at 4:00 a.m., freezing nights, empty pockets, hands numb, back aching, all before sun even rose. But inside that suffering, he held a spark. Drawing. His little escape. His secret doorway into a better world. The boy with no imagination ended up imagining worlds the world now lives in.
