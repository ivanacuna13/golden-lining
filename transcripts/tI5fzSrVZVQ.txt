Kind: captions Language: en He was fired from his own company and that rejection became his biggest blessing. The year was 1985. Jobs co-founded Apple in his garage in the 1970s and by his 20s he was a millionaire. But as Apple grew, conflict rose, Jobs bold, relentless style clashed with the company's board and CEO. Eventually, the board sided against him and Steve Jobs, the man who built Apple, was pushed out of his own company. It was public. It was humiliating and it could have been the end. But Jobs said later in a Stanford's commencement speech, "Getting fired from Apple was the best thing that could have ever happened to me." Why? Because it freed him to start over. In the years after Apple, Jobs created a new company called Next. It wasn't a commercial success, but it pushed forward powerful innovations in computing. At the same time, he purchased a small animation studio called Pixar. Pixar went on to revolutionize storytelling with films like Toy Story, Finding Nemo, and The Incredibles.
